FAQ

ChatGPT Visibility Questions, Answered

How does ChatGPT decide which businesses to recommend?

ChatGPT Search draws on a combination of its training data and real-time web browsing, weighing well-structured, well-sourced, and frequently cited content more heavily — meaning visibility depends on how clearly and credibly your business is represented across the web, not just on your own website.

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT results?

No. There is no paid placement system for ChatGPT's organic responses; visibility is earned through genuine content quality, structured data, and third-party citations, which is exactly what ChatGPT visibility optimization focuses on.

How do you test ChatGPT visibility?

SEOSERVICE.LK runs your core business queries through ChatGPT Search directly, documenting whether and how your brand is mentioned, then benchmarks the results against your top competitors to identify specific gaps.

Does this work for local Sri Lankan businesses?

Yes. ChatGPT visibility optimization works for local businesses by strengthening local citations, structured business information, and third-party mentions that help ChatGPT correctly identify and recommend businesses within a specific city or region like Colombo.
