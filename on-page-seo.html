FAQ

On-Page SEO Questions, Answered

What is on-page SEO?

On-page SEO is the practice of optimizing individual web pages — titles, headers, content, internal links, and semantic structure — so both the page's relevance and the searcher's intent are clearly matched, helping the page rank higher and get cited by AI answer engines.

How is on-page SEO different from technical SEO?

Technical SEO ensures a site can be crawled and indexed correctly, while on-page SEO optimizes the actual content and structure of individual pages — keyword targeting, headings, internal links, and search intent alignment — so those indexed pages rank for the right queries.

Does on-page SEO help with AI search results?

Yes. Clear, well-structured, intent-matched content is exactly what generative engines like Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search pull from to construct answers, making strong on-page SEO a prerequisite for AI citation.

How long does on-page SEO take to show results?

On-page SEO changes are typically re-crawled and reflected in rankings within a few weeks, with most Sri Lankan clients seeing measurable movement within 3 to 4 months as content, internal linking, and intent alignment compound.
