FAQ

International SEO Questions, Answered

What is international SEO?

International SEO is the process of optimizing a website so it ranks correctly across multiple countries or languages, using hreflang tags, regional URL structures, and localized content so each audience sees the right version of a site.

Do I need international SEO if I'm a Sri Lankan business selling abroad?

Yes. Sri Lankan exporters, e-commerce brands, and service businesses targeting buyers in the UAE, UK, US, or other markets need international SEO to avoid duplicate content issues and to ensure the right regional page ranks for each market.

What is hreflang and why does it matter?

Hreflang is an HTML attribute that tells Google which language and region a page is intended for, preventing the wrong regional page from ranking in the wrong country and avoiding duplicate content penalties across similar pages.

Should I use subdirectories or ccTLDs for international SEO?

For most Sri Lankan businesses, subdirectories (e.g. /uk/, /ae/) on the existing domain are the most practical choice — they consolidate domain authority while still allowing clear regional and hreflang targeting, without the cost of managing separate country-code domains.
