FAQ

GEO Questions, Answered

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing a brand's content, structured data, and entity signals so generative AI engines like ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity cite and recommend it directly in AI-generated answers, rather than only ranking it in traditional search results.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO optimizes for ranking position on a results page a user scrolls through; GEO optimizes for being selected as the source an AI model quotes or recommends inside a synthesized answer, which depends more on entity clarity, content structure, and citation-worthiness than on backlink volume alone.

Can I measure GEO results?

Yes. SEOSERVICE.LK tracks citation frequency and brand mentions across major AI engines including ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, benchmarked against competitors, so GEO progress is measurable rather than anecdotal.

Does GEO replace the need for SEO?

No. GEO builds on the same technical and entity foundations as SEO — sites with strong technical SEO, clear on-page structure, and established authority are the ones AI engines most often cite, so GEO and SEO should be pursued together.
