FAQ

E-Commerce SEO Questions, Answered

What is e-commerce SEO?

E-commerce SEO is the process of optimizing an online store's product pages, category pages, and technical structure so it ranks for commercial, buyer-intent search queries and converts that organic traffic into sales.

Does SEOSERVICE.LK work with Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. SEOSERVICE.LK optimizes Shopify, WooCommerce, and custom-built online stores for Sri Lankan and international e-commerce brands, covering platform-specific technical constraints, faceted navigation, and product schema.

How does e-commerce SEO handle duplicate content from filters?

Faceted navigation and product filters commonly generate thousands of near-duplicate indexed URLs. We resolve this with canonical tags, parameter handling, and strategic indexation rules that preserve crawl budget for your actual product and category pages.

What schema markup matters most for e-commerce SEO?

Product schema — including Offers, price, availability, and AggregateRating/Review data — is essential for e-commerce SEO, enabling rich results in Google Search and giving AI shopping assistants structured data to reference.
