FAQ

SEO Audit Questions, Answered

What is included in an SEO audit?

A complete SEO audit covers technical health (Core Web Vitals, crawlability, indexation), on-page factors (content, keywords, meta tags), off-page authority (backlinks, entity signals), and increasingly, AI-search readiness — whether your content is structured to be cited by generative engines.

Is the SEO audit from SEOSERVICE.LK free?

Yes, SEOSERVICE.LK offers a complimentary SEO audit covering Core Web Vitals, indexation health, schema structure, top keyword opportunities, and a competitor comparison, with no obligation to purchase further services.

How long does an SEO audit take?

SEOSERVICE.LK typically delivers a full SEO audit within 24 to 48 business hours, since each audit is manually reviewed by Buddika S. Weerasekara rather than generated purely from automated tools.

How often should a website get an SEO audit?

Most businesses benefit from a full SEO audit at least twice a year, or immediately after a major site redesign, migration, or an unexplained ranking or traffic drop.
