Before You Choose

How These Three Plans Are Actually Structured

Starter and Growth are fixed-scope, one-time projects: you pay once, the defined keyword count and deliverables are completed within one to six months, and the result is yours to keep with no ongoing fee. Enterprise is different by design — it's a monthly retainer for keywords competitive enough that the work never really finishes, which is why it comes with a dedicated strategist and bi-weekly calls rather than a fixed end date.

Generative Engine Optimization is deliberately absent from Starter. A page with a broken technical foundation won't get cited by an AI engine any more than it ranks on Google, so GEO only enters the plan once Core Web Vitals and schema are already handled — which is exactly what Starter builds first.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Are there any long-term contract lock-ins?

No. Our monthly packages operate on a month-to-month basis. We believe our ranking results should be the reason you stay, not a restrictive contract.

How do I know which plan my website needs?

Request our free audit. We will evaluate your domain authority, competitor difficulty, and search volume to recommend the exact plan that provides the highest return on investment — see how packages typically map to business size and goals in our SEO service in Sri Lanka guide.

Do you offer customized packages for large e-commerce sites?

Yes. If you have an e-commerce catalog with thousands of SKUs or multi-country operations, we create tailored custom enterprise packages.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers in LKR (for Sri Lankan businesses) and USD/EUR/AED via international wire and Stripe for overseas clients.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings?

No ethical SEO provider can guarantee specific rankings, since search algorithms are outside anyone's direct control. What each plan guarantees is the scope of work itself: the exact keyword count, deliverables and timeline listed above, delivered as specified, with progress reported against real ranking and traffic data.

What's the difference between the one-time projects and the Enterprise monthly plan?

Starter and Growth are fixed-scope projects: you pay once, the defined work is completed within one to six months, and the result is yours to keep. Enterprise is an ongoing monthly retainer built for keywords competitive enough that ranking work never really finishes, with a dedicated strategist and bi-weekly calls built into the cost.

What happens if I outgrow my current plan?

Most Starter clients who see results move to Growth once they're ready to add entity stacking and GEO, and Growth clients facing tougher competition typically move to Enterprise for ongoing monthly work. There's no penalty for switching tiers, and existing work carries over rather than restarting.

What's included in the free audit before I commit to a plan?

The same technical SEO audit described on our audits page: a full review of Core Web Vitals, crawl and indexation health, and schema gaps, delivered within 24 to 48 business hours. It's what we use to recommend a specific plan, and there's no obligation to purchase anything afterward.
