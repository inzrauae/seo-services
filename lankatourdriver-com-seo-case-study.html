The Client

Lanka Tour Driver (lankatourdriver.com)

A private driver and guided tour service for tourists visiting Sri Lanka, booked almost entirely by travelers in the UK, Europe, Australia and North America before they arrive. That means competing in international search results against global travel platforms, not just other local Sri Lankan operators.

The Approach

International Search Intent, Not Local SEO

The work was built around the specific phrases international travelers actually search before booking — "sri lanka private tours with driver", "sri lanka tour guide and driver" — rather than generic local SEO tactics that don't reach an overseas audience, backed by fast mobile performance and entity authority building targeted at those source markets.

The Result

290% More International Bookings, Global #1 in UK, US & EU Search

Figures below are Google's own numbers — two screenshots taken directly from the lankatourdriver.com Search Console property.

About This Case Study

Is this Search Console data real, or a mockup?

It's real, taken directly from the lankatourdriver.com Google Search Console property: one screenshot of the account-level Performance overview, and one of the top-queries breakdown, both for the same trailing 3-month period.

The average position shown is 17.1 — doesn't that contradict the #1 ranking claims?

No. 17.1 is the average position across every query Search Console tracked for the site over the period, which for an international tourism site includes a very long tail of location and attraction searches, such as "ridiyagama safari" (987 impressions but only 4 clicks), that the site was never specifically targeted for and ranks nowhere near #1 on. The specific commercial terms the campaign targeted, such as "lanka tour driver" and "sri lanka private tours with driver", hold the #1 position individually, as shown in the query table and verified by separate rank checks. We publish the honest account-wide average rather than hide it.

What SEO work produced this result?

Content and technical SEO built around international traveler search intent — the specific phrases visitors in the UK, Europe, Australia and North America use before booking a driver — combined with fast mobile performance and entity authority building for those source markets.
