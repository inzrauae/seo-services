What Does an Advanced SEO Company Actually Do?

A professional SEO campaign goes far beyond inserting a keyword into a page. An advanced SEO process can involve several interconnected stages.

1. SEO Audit

The campaign begins by understanding the existing website. An SEO audit may evaluate:

  • Crawlability
  • Indexability
  • Site architecture
  • URL structure
  • Metadata
  • Headings
  • Content quality
  • Internal links
  • Broken links
  • Redirects
  • Canonical URLs
  • XML sitemap
  • Robots.txt
  • Structured data
  • Mobile experience
  • Page performance
  • Core Web Vitals
  • Backlink profile
  • Competitor visibility

The purpose is not simply to generate an audit report. The purpose is to identify what should actually be fixed first.

2. Advanced Keyword Research

Keyword research is the foundation of an effective SEO campaign. However, modern keyword research should not be based purely on search volume.

For example, "SEO" is extremely broad. Compare that with "best SEO company in Sri Lanka", which has clear commercial investigation intent.

Another search, "SEO price Sri Lanka", has a different commercial intent, and "how does SEO work?" is primarily informational.

An advanced SEO strategy maps keywords according to search intent and customer journey. Keyword categories can include:

  • Informational keywords
  • Commercial keywords
  • Transactional keywords
  • Navigational keywords
  • Local keywords
  • Long-tail keywords
  • Branded keywords
  • Competitor-related keywords
  • Problem-based searches

This allows each important keyword group to be assigned to the most appropriate page.

3. Search Intent Optimization

Ranking a page for the wrong intent can produce traffic without conversions.

For example, a user searching "what is technical SEO?" is looking for information. A user searching "technical SEO company Sri Lanka" is potentially looking for a service provider. The pages should therefore be different.

An SEO company should understand: Keyword → Intent → Page → Content → Conversion. This is one of the most important principles in advanced SEO.

4. Technical SEO

Technical SEO provides the foundation for search visibility. Search engines need to discover, crawl, interpret and index your website. Technical SEO can involve:

  • Crawlability — making sure important pages can be discovered by search engine crawlers
  • Indexability — ensuring valuable pages can be indexed while unnecessary pages are appropriately controlled
  • Site architecture — creating a logical structure between categories, services, products and informational content
  • Canonicalization — helping search engines understand the preferred version of duplicate or similar URLs
  • XML sitemaps — helping search engines discover important URLs
  • Robots.txt — managing crawler access where appropriate
  • Structured data — providing machine-readable information about eligible entities, products, services and other content
  • Performance — improving website performance and overall page experience
  • Mobile SEO — ensuring the website provides a strong experience across mobile devices

Google's SEO documentation explains that SEO helps search engines understand website content and helps users find and evaluate websites through search.

5. On-Page SEO

On-page SEO involves optimizing the actual content and structure of individual pages. Important elements include SEO title, meta description, H1 heading, H2/H3 structure, keyword relevance, search intent, content depth, semantic relationships, image optimization, alt text, internal links, anchor text, URLs, structured data and conversion elements.

However, optimization should not turn content into unnatural keyword-heavy writing. Google's guidance emphasizes creating helpful, reliable, people-first content rather than content created primarily to manipulate rankings.

6. Advanced Internal Linking

Internal linking is one of the most underused areas of SEO. A website should not be a collection of isolated pages — pages should form a logical information network. For example, our own SEO Services hub connects to:

Google notes that links help users and search engines discover pages and that descriptive anchor text helps explain the destination page. This makes internal linking important for both SEO and user navigation.

7. Content Strategy

A strong SEO company does not simply publish articles for the sake of publishing articles. Content should have a purpose. A good content strategy can build topical authority around the subjects that matter to your customers — spanning SEO fundamentals, technical SEO, local SEO, commercial SEO, advanced SEO and AI search topics, so the website forms a connected topical ecosystem instead of a collection of unrelated blog posts.

8. Local SEO in Sri Lanka

For businesses targeting Sri Lankan customers, local SEO can be extremely important. A local SEO campaign can include Google Business Profile optimization, local keyword research, location pages, business information consistency, local citations, reviews, local content, local backlinks, structured data and geographic relevance.

GEO focuses on making information easier for AI-powered search systems to understand, identify and potentially reference. A GEO strategy can include clear factual content, strong entity signals, structured data, author information, organization information, expert-led content, direct answers, well-structured pages, citation-worthy information, consistent brand information and strong external authority.

Why You Should Not Choose an SEO Company Based Only on Ranking Claims

When searching for the best SEO company in Sri Lanka, businesses will encounter many providers making aggressive ranking promises. A business should be careful with statements such as "guaranteed #1 forever," "rank in 24 hours," "100% guaranteed Google ranking," "thousands of backlinks will rank you," "secret Google algorithm," or "permanent first position."

Google itself states that there are no secrets that automatically make a website rank first. A professional SEO provider should explain what will be changed, why it is being changed, how success will be measured, what risks exist, what timeframe is realistic, and how the strategy supports the business.

How to Choose the Best SEO Company in Sri Lanka

Before hiring an SEO company, ask these ten questions:

  1. Is the company legally established? Look for an identifiable company and transparent business information.
  2. Who is actually managing your SEO? Understand who will perform the work.
  3. Can they perform technical SEO? SEO should include more than content.
  4. Do they understand search intent? The right keyword is not necessarily the keyword with the highest search volume.
  5. Do they understand your industry? Industry knowledge improves strategic decision-making.
  6. How do they approach internal linking? Internal architecture is an important part of scalable SEO.
  7. What is their content strategy? Ask what content will be created and why.
  8. How do they approach backlinks? Prioritize sustainable, relevant authority-building methods.
  9. How do they report results? You should be able to see what is improving.
  10. Do they connect SEO with business outcomes? The ultimate goal is not a ranking screenshot — it's business growth.
