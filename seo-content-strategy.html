FAQ

SEO Content Strategy Questions, Answered

What is an SEO content strategy?

An SEO content strategy is a structured plan for what content to publish, when, and how it interlinks, built around topic clusters and search intent so a website builds recognized topical authority over time instead of publishing disconnected, one-off articles.

What is topical authority in SEO?

Topical authority is the depth and breadth of coverage a website demonstrates on a specific subject. Google rewards sites that comprehensively cover a topic through interlinked content clusters with higher trust and rankings than sites with scattered, shallow coverage.

How does content strategy support Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

AI answer engines favor content that gives clear, direct, well-structured answers to specific questions. An SEO content strategy built around real search questions — with concise, quotable answers — significantly increases the chance of being cited inside AI Overviews and chat-based search results.

How often should a business publish SEO content?

Consistency matters more than frequency. Most Sri Lankan businesses see stronger results from publishing 2 to 4 well-researched, intent-matched pieces per month within a clear topic cluster than from sporadic high-volume publishing with no strategic structure.
