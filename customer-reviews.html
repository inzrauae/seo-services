About These Reviews, Answered

Are these reviews independently verified by a third party?

Not by an external review platform at this point; they're direct feedback shared by clients with permission to publish. If you'd rather read reviews verified by Google itself, our Google Business Profile is linked below and will show any reviews left there directly.

How can I leave a review?

The fastest way is through our Google Business Profile, linked below on this page, where you can post directly and anyone can see it's genuinely tied to your Google account. We also welcome feedback by WhatsApp or email if that's easier.

Do you have reviews in Sinhala?

Not published yet. Every client conversation we've had permission to quote so far happened in English, even with Sri Lankan clients, so that's what's shown here. If you're a client who'd rather leave feedback in Sinhala, we'd genuinely like to include it; message us and we'll add it properly credited.

Why are some reviews from clients outside Sri Lanka?

Alongside Sri Lankan clients, we work directly with businesses in the UK, Australia, Canada, the UAE, the US and Singapore, which is reflected in both the case studies and the feedback here. The technical SEO and GEO methodology doesn't change by market.

Do you remove negative reviews?

Nothing has been removed from this page. If a client's feedback pointed at something we genuinely got wrong, the honest response is to fix the issue and show that fix elsewhere on the site, not to hide the criticism.

Can I see results, not just quotes, from these clients?

Yes, six of these engagements have full documented case studies with the actual keyword rankings and traffic figures, not just a quote. See the case studies page for POS retail, construction, tourism, corporate merchandise, vehicle rental and apparel clients specifically.
