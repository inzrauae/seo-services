FAQ

Technical SEO Questions, Answered

What does technical SEO include?

Technical SEO includes Core Web Vitals speed optimization, crawlability and indexation fixes, XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration, canonicalization, structured data (JSON-LD schema), mobile rendering, and site architecture — the foundation search engines need to properly access and understand a website.

Why is technical SEO important for ranking in Sri Lanka?

Google prioritizes fast, crawlable, error-free websites in its rankings. Many Sri Lankan business websites lose visibility due to slow load times, broken indexation, or missing schema — technical SEO fixes these blockers so on-page and content work can actually convert into ranking gains.

How long does a technical SEO audit take?

SEOSERVICE.LK typically delivers a full technical SEO audit within 24 to 48 business hours, covering Core Web Vitals, crawl errors, indexation status, and schema gaps, followed by a prioritized fix roadmap.

Can technical SEO fix a sudden ranking drop?

Yes. Sudden ranking drops are frequently caused by technical issues such as accidental noindex tags, broken redirects, server errors, or Core Web Vitals degradation. A technical SEO audit identifies and resolves these root causes.
