FAQ

Off-Page SEO Questions, Answered

What is off-page SEO?

Off-page SEO covers everything that builds a website's authority outside the site itself — backlinks from trusted publications, brand mentions, digital PR, and entity signals that establish your brand as a credible, recognized source in Google's Knowledge Graph.

What is Google Entity Stacking?

Google Entity Stacking is the practice of connecting a brand's profiles, citations, and structured data across the web and Google's own properties so Google recognizes it as a single, disambiguated, authoritative entity — creating durable rankings that resist algorithm updates better than backlinks alone.

Is off-page SEO safe, or does it risk a Google penalty?

SEOSERVICE.LK only uses 100% white-hat off-page methods — no PBNs, no spammy link farms, and no paid link schemes. Every backlink and entity signal is built to withstand Google's core algorithm updates rather than risk a manual penalty.

How many backlinks does a Sri Lankan website need to rank?

There is no fixed number — link building is judged on relevance and authority, not volume. A handful of high-DR, topically relevant editorial links from real publications outperforms hundreds of low-quality directory links.
