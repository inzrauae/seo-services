How to Target UK and UAE Customers From Sri Lanka

Selling overseas is mostly an SEO structure problem. Here is how Sri Lankan businesses can reach buyers in the UK and the UAE.

By Buddhika S Weerasekara · 14 October 2026

Quick Answer

To reach UK or UAE customers from Sri Lanka, research how people there search, choose a clear site structure (subfolder, subdomain or country domain), localise currency, contact details and wording, add hreflang if you serve several regions, and build links and mentions from sites that matter in those markets.

Start with how people in that market search

A phrase that works in Sri Lanka may not match what buyers in London or Dubai type. Start with keyword research for each market separately. Our work for The Merch List in Dubai and Vapourz Lounge in London both began this way.

Choose a site structure

You can serve another country from a subfolder on your main site, a subdomain or a separate country domain. Subfolders are the easiest to launch because they share the main site's authority. The trade-offs are explained in our international SEO guide, and the international SEO service covers implementation.

Localise the details buyers notice

  • Show prices in the local currency (pounds or dirhams).
  • Give shipping, returns and contact details that make sense in that market.
  • Use local spelling and vocabulary, not just a translation.
  • Add trust signals buyers expect, such as clear returns and real reviews.

Tell Google which version is for whom

If you run several regional versions, use hreflang so Google shows the right one. Done wrong, it causes more problems than it solves, so check it carefully. If you are changing structure at the same time, follow the website migration SEO checklist to avoid losing rankings.

Build authority where your buyers are

Links and mentions from sites that matter in the UK or the UAE carry more weight there than a pile of unrelated links. See link building in 2026 and our link building service. For online stores, the e-commerce SEO checklist and e-commerce SEO service cover product and category pages.

Remember tourists search from abroad too

Travel businesses sell to overseas visitors before they land. Lanka Tour Driver targets travellers searching from the UK, US and EU. If you serve tourists, local SEO and international SEO work together.

How AI engines answer in each market

People in London and Dubai increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews for recommendations. These engines tailor answers to the searcher's language and often their location, so a Sri Lankan business has to be clearly identifiable in the market it wants. That is Generative Engine Optimization for international audiences, and it starts with consistent business details, clear service descriptions and pages written for that market.

Practically, state exactly where you ship, which currency you use and how returns work, in plain sentences an engine can quote. Mention the market by name on the relevant pages. Earn mentions on sites that matter to buyers there, and add structured data so your products and business are described accurately. Our AI search optimization service covers this alongside classic rankings.

A checklist before you launch in a new market

  • Keyword research done for that country, not copied from Sri Lanka.
  • Prices, shipping and returns shown in local terms.
  • A clear site structure decided and documented.
  • Pages written in the vocabulary buyers there actually use.
  • Structured data and business details consistent everywhere.
  • A plan for earning links and mentions from sites in that market.

Common mistakes when selling abroad

The most common mistake is translating a Sri Lankan site word for word and expecting it to rank abroad. The second is launching a separate domain without the authority or budget to support it. The third is adding hreflang tags without understanding them, which can send visitors to the wrong version. The fourth is ignoring trust: overseas buyers want clear returns, real reviews and visible contact details before they spend money.

What to do in the first 90 days

In the first month, research the market and decide your structure. In the second, build or adapt your key pages with local currency, shipping and returns, and add structured data. In the third, start earning links and mentions from sites that matter to buyers there, and begin tracking both classic rankings and whether AI engines mention you for market-specific questions. Review the results at the end of the quarter and double down on what is working. Selling abroad is a long game, so set expectations accordingly and measure progress against a baseline.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a separate website for the UK or UAE?

Not necessarily. Many businesses start with a subfolder on one site, adding a separate domain only when the market justifies it.

Can I rank in the UK from Sri Lanka?

Yes. Search engines care about relevance and authority for the searcher's market, not where the site owner lives. Local trust signals and links in that market help.

Is hreflang required?

Only when you have near-identical pages for different regions or languages. A single-region site does not need it.
