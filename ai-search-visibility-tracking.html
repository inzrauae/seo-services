FAQ

AI Search Visibility Tracking Questions, Answered

What is AI search visibility tracking?

AI search visibility tracking is the ongoing process of monitoring how often, how prominently, and how favorably a brand is mentioned or cited across AI-powered search platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, benchmarked against competitors.

How is this different from traditional rank tracking?

Traditional rank tracking measures position on a search results page; AI search visibility tracking measures whether and how a brand appears inside AI-generated answers, which involves citation frequency and sentiment rather than a numbered ranking position.

How often should AI visibility be tracked?

SEOSERVICE.LK recommends monthly tracking at minimum, since AI platforms update their models and retrieval behavior frequently enough that visibility can shift meaningfully month to month.

Can tracking data actually improve my AI visibility?

Yes. Tracking data identifies exactly which queries and platforms are underperforming, which directly informs where to focus GEO, content, and citation-building efforts for the highest impact.
