FAQ

Link Building Questions, Answered

What is link building in SEO?

Link building is the process of acquiring hyperlinks from other websites that point back to your own, signaling to Google that other sites vouch for your content's relevance and trustworthiness — a major factor in how highly a page ranks.

Is link building still important with AI search on the rise?

Yes. Backlinks remain a core trust and authority signal for both traditional Google rankings and the entity credibility that AI answer engines look for when deciding which sources to cite as authoritative.

What kind of backlinks does SEOSERVICE.LK build?

SEOSERVICE.LK builds high-authority, topically relevant editorial backlinks through digital PR and outreach to real publications — never PBNs, paid link networks, or spammy directory submissions that risk a Google penalty.

Can bad backlinks hurt my rankings?

Yes. Spammy or toxic backlinks — from link farms, unrelated foreign domains, or manipulative schemes — can trigger a Google manual action or algorithmic devaluation. A backlink audit and disavow process identifies and neutralizes these risks.
