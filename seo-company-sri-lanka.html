FAQ

SEO Company in Sri Lanka: Common Questions

What does an SEO company in Sri Lanka do?

An SEO company improves your website so it ranks higher on Google and gets cited in AI search. In practice that means fixing technical problems, optimizing pages for the keywords your customers use, building trusted links, and reporting on the enquiries that result.

How much does an SEO company in Sri Lanka charge?

Our packages are fixed-scope projects: Starter is Rs. 60,000, Growth is Rs. 99,999 and Enterprise is Rs. 120,000, all one-time with no hidden fees. Other agencies often charge monthly retainers, so compare what is included, not just the headline number.

How long does it take for an SEO company to show results?

Technical fixes can show movement in four to eight weeks, low-competition and local terms typically take three to six months, and competitive national terms usually take six to twelve months of sustained work. Any company promising page one in a few weeks is not being honest with you.

Can an SEO company guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

No. Google decides rankings and nobody outside Google can guarantee a position. What a good SEO company can guarantee is the work it will do, the reporting you will receive and the standards it follows. Be cautious of anyone who guarantees a ranking.

Do you work with businesses outside Colombo?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in all 25 districts of Sri Lanka, and with Sri Lankan brands targeting the UAE, UK and other markets. Audits, strategy calls and reporting all happen online.

How do I choose the right SEO company in Sri Lanka?

Ask for named clients and case studies you can verify, a written scope of what is done each month, reporting based on enquiries rather than vanity metrics, and a clear explanation of how links are built. Our guide to the best SEO company in Sri Lanka gives a full scoring checklist.
