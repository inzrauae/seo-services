FAQ

Local SEO Questions, Answered

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the practice of optimizing a business's online presence so it appears prominently in local search results and Google's Map Pack for nearby customers — covering Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, reviews, and geo-targeted content.

How do I rank in Google's local 3-pack in Sri Lanka?

Ranking in Google's local 3-pack requires a fully optimized Google Business Profile, consistent NAP (name, address, phone) citations across the web, positive review velocity, and geo-relevant on-site content — all weighted by Google's proximity, relevance, and prominence signals.

Does local SEO work for businesses outside Colombo?

Yes. SEOSERVICE.LK builds local SEO campaigns for businesses across Sri Lanka, including Kandy, Galle, Jaffna, and other districts, using geo-targeted landing pages and localized Google Business Profile optimization.

How important are Google reviews for local ranking?

Google reviews are a major local ranking signal, affecting both visibility in the Map Pack and click-through rate. Review count, recency, rating, and owner responses all factor into how Google ranks a business locally.
