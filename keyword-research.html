High Search Volume Doesn't Mean High Value

FAQ

Keyword Research Questions, Answered

What is keyword research in SEO?

Keyword research is the process of identifying the exact terms and questions a target audience searches for, then evaluating each by search volume, competition, and intent so content strategy can prioritize the opportunities most likely to drive rankings and revenue.

How is keyword research different for AI search engines?

AI search and answer engines respond to natural-language, conversational queries rather than short fragment keywords. Modern keyword research must map both traditional search terms and full-sentence questions people ask AI tools like ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews.

How many keywords should a small business target?

Most Sri Lankan small businesses see the best return starting with a focused set of 10 to 20 high-intent keyword clusters rather than spreading thin across hundreds of low-value terms — quality and intent match outperform sheer keyword count.

Does keyword research include competitor analysis?

Yes. A thorough keyword research process includes analyzing which keywords competitors already rank for, identifying gaps in their content, and finding underserved opportunities a business can realistically win.
