What is search engine optimization and how does it work in Sri Lanka?

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the process of improving a website so it ranks higher in Google for the terms your customers actually search. For a practical breakdown of how it works, what it costs, and realistic timelines for Sri Lankan businesses, see our complete SEO Sri Lanka guide.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most clients begin seeing measurable movement in rankings and traffic within 3–4 months, with significant compounding results by month 6–9, depending on competition and starting point.

Do you guarantee first page rankings?

No ethical SEO provider can guarantee specific rankings — search algorithms are outside anyone's direct control. What we guarantee is a transparent, data-backed process built to maximize your visibility sustainably.

What makes Entity Stacking different from typical SEO?

Entity Stacking builds your brand's recognition as a trusted "entity" across Google's Knowledge Graph and third-party platforms, reinforcing topical authority beyond just on-site optimization.

Do you work with businesses outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. While we specialize in the Sri Lankan market, we also work with international clients targeting English-language and regional search markets.

What's included in the free audit?

What SEO services does SEOSERVICE.LK provide?

What's the difference between an SEO agency and an SEO specialist?

An SEO agency often spreads your account across a team and an account manager. Working with a dedicated SEO specialist means you deal directly with the person doing the strategy and execution — which is how we operate as your SEO company.

How is SEO marketing different from paid ads?

SEO marketing builds organic, compounding visibility in Google search results, while paid ads buy temporary placement. A strong SEO marketing strategy reduces your long-term reliance on ad spend.

What does Google SEO actually involve?

Google SEO covers everything Google's ranking systems evaluate — Core Web Vitals, crawlability, content quality and relevance, structured data, and off-site authority signals like backlinks and entity recognition.
