How This Page Is Organized

SEO or GEO? How These 19 Services Fit Together

The 19 services above split into two groups that solve two different problems. Ten are foundational SEO — technical SEO, on-page, off-page, local, e-commerce, international, audits, keyword research, content strategy and link building — and they exist to get a URL ranked in Google's list of links. Nine are Generative Engine Optimization and its specializations, and they exist to get a brand quoted directly inside an AI-generated answer, which depends on entity clarity and extractable content far more than on link position.

In practice almost no serious plan is purely one or the other. A page with a broken technical foundation won't get cited by an AI engine either, and a perfectly optimized page that no answer engine can identify as belonging to a real, verifiable business won't be trusted enough to quote. Our Growth and Enterprise packages combine both deliberately: Growth adds Google Entity Stacking and GEO on top of the technical and on-page work in Starter, and Enterprise runs both as an ongoing monthly programme rather than a one-time project.

FAQ

Choosing Between These Services, Answered

Do I need all 19 of these services, or can I start with just one?

No client needs every service at once. Most engagements start with a technical SEO audit, which identifies which of the 19 capabilities will move the needle fastest for that specific site, then builds a phased plan from there rather than selling the full catalog upfront.

What's the actual difference between the SEO services and the GEO services here?

The 10 SEO services (technical, on-page, off-page, local, and so on) are built to rank a URL in Google's list of links. The 9 GEO services are built to get a brand quoted directly inside an AI-generated answer, which depends more on entity clarity and extractable content than on link position.

Can SEO and GEO services be combined into one plan?

Yes. The Growth and Enterprise packages already combine them: Growth adds Google Entity Stacking and Generative Engine Optimization on top of the technical and on-page work in Starter, and Enterprise runs both as an ongoing monthly programme rather than a one-time project.

How long does a technical SEO audit actually take?

A full technical SEO audit is typically delivered within 24 to 48 business hours, covering Core Web Vitals, crawl and indexation issues, schema gaps, and a prioritized list of what to fix first. It is the standard starting point before any of the 19 services get scoped into a plan.

How long before I see ranking results from these services?

It depends on competition and the site's starting condition. Technical fixes can show measurable movement in four to eight weeks, low-competition and local terms typically take three to six months, and competitive national terms, the kind contested in Colombo, usually take six to twelve months of sustained work.

Are these services available outside Colombo?

Yes, all 19 services are delivered remotely to businesses across all 25 districts of Sri Lanka, from Jaffna to Galle. Audits, strategy calls and reporting all happen online, so a business in Matara or Trincomalee gets the same service as one based in Colombo.

What happens after I request the free audit?

The audit is reviewed personally rather than run through an automated report generator, and delivered within 24 to 48 business hours with a prioritized list of issues. There is no obligation to buy a package afterward; the audit itself is the deliverable, and the roadmap conversation only happens if it's useful to you.

Can I mix services from different categories, like Local SEO with AI Citation Optimization?

Yes. The 19 services on this page are building blocks, not a fixed bundle, and a plan for a local retailer typically looks nothing like one for an e-commerce brand chasing AI citations. The free audit is what determines which specific combination fits a given business.
