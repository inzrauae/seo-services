How to Build Backlinks in Sri Lanka: Directories, PR and Partnerships

Backlinks help most when they are earned from relevant, trustworthy sources. This guide covers reputable Sri Lankan directories, digital PR, partnerships and guest contributions, how to judge link quality, and a 30-day plan you can follow.

By Buddhika S Weerasekara, Founder & CEO · 17 October 2026

Quick Answer

To build backlinks in Sri Lanka, define your priority pages, then earn relevant links through reputable business directories, digital PR, genuine partnerships, careful guest contributions and useful resources other sites want to cite. Judge each opportunity on relevance, editorial quality and real audience, avoid bulk or paid link schemes, and track results. No single link guarantees a ranking.

What are backlinks and why do they matter?

A backlink is a link from another website to your website. Search engines can use links to discover pages and understand relationships between websites. Links may also help people find your business through relevant articles, directories, recommendations and industry resources.

Backlinks can contribute to SEO when they are earned naturally from relevant, trustworthy sources. However, not every backlink has the same value, and no individual link guarantees improved rankings.

A relevant mention of a Sri Lankan construction company in a reputable property or construction publication, for example, may do more to reach its target audience than a link from an unrelated directory created only to sell backlinks.

1. Start with a backlink strategy for your business

Before contacting any website, define what you want your backlink campaign to achieve. Your strategy should reflect your industry, target customers, location, website content and available resources.

Identify your target audience

A business serving customers across Sri Lanka may benefit from national business publications, industry websites and relevant professional organisations. A business serving a specific town may also prioritise local directories, community publications, chambers of commerce and nearby business partners.

Choose the pages you want to promote

Decide which pages deserve attention. These might include:

  • Your main service pages.
  • Useful guides and educational articles.
  • Original research, surveys or industry reports.
  • Case studies demonstrating real results.
  • Tools, calculators and other resources people may want to reference.

Do not point every backlink at your homepage. Link destinations should make sense in the context of the referring page, and the content should give its audience value.

2. Build backlinks through reputable Sri Lankan business directories

Business directories help customers discover companies and verify basic business information. Some also provide a backlink, although the SEO value of directory links varies and many listings use nofollow or other link attributes.

The main objective should be accurate business representation and genuine visibility, not submitting your website to as many directories as possible.

Which types of directories should you consider?

  • Established local business directories: listings that people genuinely use to find companies and services.
  • Chambers of commerce: relevant local or national business organisations, where membership and listing eligibility apply.
  • Industry directories: directories dedicated to your field, such as construction, tourism, technology, retail or professional services.
  • Professional associations: membership directories for businesses or professionals who meet the organisation's criteria.
  • Supplier and partner directories: listings offered by genuine manufacturers, distributors, resellers or business partners.

How to evaluate a directory

Before submitting your business, review the directory's website and ask:

  • Does it serve Sri Lankan customers or your specific industry?
  • Does it have genuine listings and useful information?
  • Are the listed businesses relevant and credible?
  • Does it moderate submissions and remove obvious spam?
  • Is the listing process transparent about fees and requirements?
  • Would the listing still be useful if it gave no SEO benefit?

Be cautious of directories that accept every submission without review, contain unrelated businesses from many countries, or promise guaranteed rankings through paid listings.

Keep your business information consistent

Use accurate and consistent information for your business name, address, phone number, website and service categories. If your business has several branches, represent each location correctly and follow the directory's rules. Consistent listings also support your local SEO, your Google Business Profile and your brand entity.

Use a real business email and accurate descriptions. Do not create fake locations or duplicate listings to target additional keywords.

3. Earn backlinks through digital PR

Digital PR means creating newsworthy information, stories, resources or expert commentary that relevant publications and websites may want to cover. It can earn editorial mentions, referral traffic, brand awareness and backlinks.

Unlike bulk link submission, digital PR depends on giving publishers a genuine reason to reference your business or content.

Digital PR ideas for Sri Lankan businesses

  • Original research: publish a survey or analysis of an industry trend using a clear and honest methodology.
  • Expert commentary: offer useful insights on topics within your professional expertise.
  • Business milestones: share meaningful launches, expansions or projects with genuine news value.
  • Community initiatives: document real business participation in local events, training or community projects.
  • Industry reports: turn relevant data into a useful report with findings that journalists, associations and businesses can reference.
  • Practical resources: create guides, statistics, visual explanations or tools that solve a problem for a specific audience.

Find publications that cover your industry

Research Sri Lankan news outlets, business publications, trade websites and local publications that regularly cover your sector. Review their recent articles to understand the subjects they report on and the type of information they use.

Look for a relevant editor, journalist or contributor contact. Follow the publication's submission guidelines and pitch a story that fits its audience instead of sending a generic request for a backlink.

What to include in a PR pitch

  • A specific and relevant subject line.
  • A concise explanation of why the story matters now.
  • Original findings, useful data or a clear expert perspective.
  • Evidence that supports your claims.
  • A link to the supporting resource or source material.
  • Accurate contact details for follow-up questions.

Coverage is never guaranteed. A successful pitch prioritises news value and usefulness, not a demand for an exact anchor text or a dofollow link.

4. Build links through genuine business partnerships

Partnerships can create natural opportunities for businesses to mention one another online. These links are most useful when they reflect a real professional relationship and help customers discover relevant services.

Potential partnership opportunities

  • Suppliers and distributors: ask whether they maintain a legitimate partner, dealer or reseller directory.
  • Complementary service providers: collaborate with businesses that serve the same audience without offering identical services.
  • Trade associations: participate in relevant industry groups and contribute useful resources.
  • Event organisers: support legitimate conferences, training sessions, workshops or community events that publish sponsor or participant information.
  • Educational institutions: contribute expertise to genuine educational projects, guest sessions or industry resources when there is a relevant opportunity.
  • Professional networks: collaborate on useful research, interviews, case studies or practical guides.

Make the partnership useful for both sides

Instead of asking a business to add a link without context, propose something useful. You could contribute a technical guide, share relevant expertise, create a joint case study or develop a resource that benefits both audiences.

A web development company and a digital marketing consultancy, for example, could jointly publish a guide explaining how website performance affects online visibility. Each business can reference the resource where it is genuinely relevant.

Avoid exchanging links at scale just to manipulate rankings. Search engine guidelines may treat excessive reciprocal linking and link schemes as spam.

5. Use guest contributions carefully

Guest contributions can introduce your expertise to a new audience and create relationships with relevant publishers. They are most valuable when the publication has real readers and the contribution adds original information.

How to find suitable guest contribution opportunities

  1. Identify publications that cover your industry or target audience.
  2. Read their submission guidelines and recent articles.
  3. Find a topic that fills a genuine information gap.
  4. Pitch a clear outline or a few original ideas.
  5. Write a useful, accurate contribution that meets editorial standards.
  6. Include an author biography or website link only where the publisher permits it and it is appropriate.

Do not use guest posting as a mass link-placement scheme. Avoid websites that exist mainly to publish paid articles with keyword-rich backlinks and little editorial oversight.

If payment or sponsorship is involved, follow the publisher's disclosure requirements and applicable search engine link qualification guidelines. Paid links intended to influence rankings should be appropriately qualified, for example with rel="sponsored" where applicable.

6. Create resources other websites want to reference

One sustainable approach to link building is to publish something other websites find genuinely useful. Instead of requesting every link individually, create resources that make it easier for people to reference your work. Our SEO content strategy service plans exactly this kind of content.

Resources that can attract links

  • Original industry statistics and survey results.
  • Detailed guides that explain complex tasks clearly, such as our technical SEO audit checklist.
  • Free calculators, checklists, templates and practical tools, such as our free SEO checker.
  • Well-documented case studies and research reports.
  • Visual explanations that clarify difficult concepts.
  • Curated resources with clear selection criteria and regular maintenance.

For a Sri Lankan audience, a resource should address a real local need when local relevance matters. That could involve local business practices, industry terminology, practical cost comparisons with transparent assumptions, or data collected from a clearly defined Sri Lankan sample.

Do not present estimates as verified market data, and do not publish private customer information without permission.

7. Evaluate backlink quality before spending time or money

Backlink metrics from SEO tools can help prioritise research, but no single score fully measures the quality of a website or predicts the effect of a link.

Assess the website itself, the linking page, the context of the link and the audience it reaches.

A practical backlink evaluation checklist

  • Relevance: does the website or page relate to your industry, topic, audience or location?
  • Editorial quality: is the content useful, accurate and meaningfully maintained?
  • Real audience: does the site appear to serve readers rather than exist solely to sell links?
  • Placement: does the link appear naturally within relevant content or a legitimate business listing?
  • Link attributes: is the link editorial, sponsored, nofollow or otherwise qualified?
  • Indexability: can the relevant page be accessed and potentially indexed?
  • Spam signals: does the site publish unrelated content, excessive outbound links or obvious link-selling pages?
  • Business value: could the link generate referral visits, useful relationships or brand exposure?

Third-party metrics such as Domain Authority, Domain Rating and Authority Score are estimates created by their respective providers. They are not Google ranking factors, and a high score alone does not make a website a good link source. 8. Use natural anchor text

Anchor text is the clickable text of a link. It gives readers and search engines context about the destination.

Use anchor text that accurately describes the linked page and fits naturally within the sentence. Depending on the context, this may be your business name, the page title, a descriptive phrase or a relevant topic.

A relevant publication might link to an SEO resource using the company's name or wording such as "guide to link building". Both can be appropriate when they accurately describe the destination.

Avoid repeatedly requesting the exact same commercial keyword across unrelated websites. Large-scale, unnatural use of keyword-rich anchors can be a sign of manipulative link building. The same principle applies to links inside your own site; see our internal linking guide.

You do not need to engineer a perfect anchor-text percentage. Focus on earning links that make sense to publishers and readers.

9. Track backlink acquisition and results

Keep a simple record of your outreach and the links you earn. It shows which activities produce useful relationships and where to focus your time.

What to track

  • Referring website and linking page URL.
  • Target page on your website.
  • Link acquisition method, such as a directory, PR mention or partnership.
  • Publication or listing date.
  • Anchor text and link attributes, where available.
  • Referral traffic and relevant enquiries, if measurable.
  • Organic search performance of the linked page over time.
  • Outreach status and follow-up date.

Use the links report in Google Search Console and suitable backlink analysis tools to review your link profile. These reports may not show every link, and ranking changes have many possible causes. Avoid assuming that one new backlink caused a ranking change without further evidence.

10. Common link-building mistakes to avoid

  • Buying bulk backlinks: large packages of low-quality links create spam risks and are rarely a reliable strategy.
  • Submitting to every directory: irrelevant listings add little value and waste time.
  • Prioritising authority scores alone: a high third-party score does not guarantee relevance, editorial quality or referral value.
  • Using automated link schemes: scaled link placement and manipulative tactics may violate search engine spam policies.
  • Over-optimising anchor text: repeated exact-match commercial anchors can look unnatural.
  • Publishing low-value guest posts: content created mainly to place a link can undermine the campaign.
  • Ignoring the linked page: backlinks are less useful when the destination is outdated, irrelevant or poor quality.
  • Expecting immediate rankings: link acquisition does not guarantee rapid or measurable ranking improvements.

11. A simple 30-day backlink plan for Sri Lankan businesses

A focused plan is often more manageable than trying to acquire dozens of links at once. Adapt the workload to your industry, available resources and the quality of opportunities you can find.

Week 1: research and preparation

  • Review your existing backlinks and identify priority pages.
  • Build a shortlist of relevant directories, industry organisations, publications and business partners.
  • Evaluate each opportunity for relevance, credibility and likely audience value.

Week 2: improve link-worthy content

  • Improve one useful guide, case study or resource on your website.
  • Check its accuracy, usability and internal links.
  • Prepare a short explanation of why the resource is useful to a particular audience.

Week 3: outreach and partnerships

  • Complete eligible, reputable business directory listings.
  • Contact a small number of highly relevant publications or partners with personalised proposals.
  • Offer useful information or collaboration instead of demanding a backlink.

Week 4: follow-up and measurement

  • Follow up politely where appropriate.
  • Record responses, successful placements and lessons learned.
  • Review referral traffic, link quality and the performance of priority pages.
  • Use the findings to plan the next month of outreach.

No universal number of backlinks suits every business each month. A few relevant editorial links can be worth more than many low-quality placements. Prioritise sustainable progress and genuine business value.

Conclusion

Building backlinks in Sri Lanka is not simply about submitting a website to directories or buying links. Sustainable link acquisition combines relevant business listings, useful content, credible digital PR and genuine professional relationships.

Start by understanding your audience, improving the pages you want people to discover, and identifying websites that have a legitimate reason to reference your business. Evaluate opportunities carefully, avoid manipulative tactics and measure outcomes over time.

FAQ

Common Questions

How do I build backlinks in Sri Lanka?

Start with a strategy and priority pages, then earn links through reputable local and industry directories, digital PR, genuine business partnerships, careful guest contributions and useful resources other sites want to reference. Prioritise relevance and quality over volume.

Are Sri Lankan business directories good for SEO?

Some help customers find you and may provide a link, but their SEO value varies and many use nofollow attributes. Choose directories with real audiences and moderated, relevant listings, and keep your business details consistent.

Is buying backlinks safe?

Paid links intended to influence rankings can violate search engine spam policies and should be avoided or properly qualified, for example with rel="sponsored". Bulk low-quality link packages carry spam risk and rarely work as a reliable strategy.

How many backlinks do I need?

There is no universal number. A few relevant editorial links from credible sites can be worth more than many low-quality placements, so focus on sustainable, relevant links and measure their effect on the linked pages.

How long do backlinks take to improve rankings?

Link acquisition does not guarantee quick or measurable ranking gains, and rankings depend on many factors. Track referral traffic, indexing and the linked page's organic performance over time instead of expecting an immediate change.
